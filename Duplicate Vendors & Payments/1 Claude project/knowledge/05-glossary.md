# Glossary

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Supplier record** | One entry in the supplier list, with its own number |
| **Blocked** | A record no one may order from; the supplier list gives the date it was blocked |
| **Pair** | Two supplier records compared with each other |
| **Plausible pair** | Two records sharing a tax number, a bank account or the first word of their name: the only pairs compared |
| **Match score** | 0 to 1: tax number 0.45, bank account 0.35, near-identical name up to 0.15, same city 0.05 |
| **Policy line** | The score at which a pair goes to a person: 0.35 here, set by policy |
| **Review queue** | The pairs at or above the policy line |
| **Look-alike** | A pair with near-identical names and nothing else in common |
| **Evidence** | The two records side by side, field by field, with their orders, their spend and any orders for the same amount on both |
| **Same-amount orders** | An order on each record of a pair for the same amount, to the cent, within 30 days of each other |
| **Assessment** | A short read of a pair, citing only its evidence, with a recommendation and a confidence |
| **The same supplier / Different suppliers / Refer it on** | The three decisions a person can make on a pair. Where the pair shares a bank account but not a tax number, *Different suppliers · bank details verified* replaces the plain option |
| **Decision card** | The form in the chat where one person decides every pair, with a reason for each, their full name and their role |
| **Delegation of authority** | Who may decide the pairs and who may approve the release: never the same people, never the same roles |
| **Forensic review** | A specialist look at shared bank details, never a verdict |
| **Paid twice** | Same-amount orders on both records of a pair confirmed as one supplier, both paid |
| **Possible duplicate** | The same match on a pair referred on or decided as different suppliers: listed, never counted as paid twice |
| **At stake** | Spend the recommended actions protect: shown, never counted as a gain |
| **Change request** | A draft for the supplier-list owner: both records of each pair, what to do and why. Nothing is merged |
| **Approval to release** | A second person's approval, in a senior role, before findings go anywhere |
| **Audit trail** | Every step, decision and control of the review, with who, when and why, filed as a workbook |
| **Fingerprint** | A short code that changes if the figures change: proof of exactly what was seen or approved |
| **Tamper check** | Confirms the record of the review hasn't been edited, removed or reordered |
