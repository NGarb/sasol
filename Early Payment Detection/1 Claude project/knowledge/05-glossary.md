# Glossary

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Payment period** | The days from receiving a valid invoice to when payment is due, as the agreement sets them |
| **Annexure** | A later addition to an agreement, such as one that amends the payment period. An agreement may say an annexure prevails where it conflicts, and that an amendment needs both parties' signatures |
| **Side letter** | A later letter that changes one term, such as the settlement discount or its window |
| **From statement** | A payment period counted from the supplier's month-end statement: the last day of the invoice's month, plus the period |
| **Words and figures** | A period written both ways, in figures with the words in brackets. Where they disagree, a person decides which governs |
| **Qualifying small enterprise** | A small business under the Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment Codes of Good Practice. Paying one sooner than usual can be part of a purchaser's enterprise and supplier development commitment |
| **Read two ways** | An agreement whose payment terms support two readings. A person decides which governs, or refers it on |
| **Settlement discount** | A percentage off the invoice for paying within a set window |
| **Discount taken** | Paid inside the window: correct behaviour, never a finding |
| **Discount not taken** | A discount was on offer, but the payment came after the window. Valued at the discount that was missed |
| **Paid early** | Paid more than five days before the due date, outside any discount window. Valued as the cost of releasing the cash early |
| **On time** | Paid within five days either side of the due date |
| **Paid late** | Paid more than five days after the due date. Counted, not valued |
| **Treasury rate** | The yearly cost of short-term funding used to value early payments: 9.5% here, a stated assumption, not the cost of capital |
| **Termination notice** | The notice needed to end an agreement, not the notice a supplier gives before changing its rates |
| **Independent check** | A second reading of every term, done a different way, which flags any difference |
| **Your decision** | A judgement only a person can make, recorded with the reason, their full name and their role |
| **Delegation of authority** | Which roles may decide agreement terms, and which may approve the release. The two never overlap |
| **Refer it on** | Passing a decision to someone with the authority to make it; the agreement is set aside and listed |
| **Approval to release** | A different person's approval, in a release role, before anything is filed |
| **Fingerprint** | A short code that changes if the figures change: proof of exactly what was seen or approved |
| **Record of the review** | Every step, in order, each linked to the one before |
| **Tamper check** | Confirms the record hasn't been edited, removed or reordered |
| **Evidence pack** | The Excel workbook with every finding, term, decision and recommendation behind the figures |
| **Audit trail** | The Excel workbook that sets out every step and decision, who made it and why, how each figure was worked out, and every control |
| **Data** | The Excel workbook with the payment history as read and every payment's outcome, for your own analysis |
| **Management pack** | The management briefing (Word) and the presentation (PowerPoint) filed with the release, for the next meeting |
